above the composer's top rule. the ready chevron and ordinary footer remain
visible. skid reports `idle/none/none` with a draft. guarded send refuses the
draft, but the status omits the open picker and an idle wait can match.

suggestion list against ordinary slash drafts and quoted lists before changing
how it affects interaction and activity. resolution requires those distinctions
